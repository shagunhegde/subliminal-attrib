# Attribution: everything run, and every number

Branch `pivot`. Run dir on the pod: `runs/pivot-ce60f7b48648/`
Data dir: `runs/pivot-data-cb8e8cccd897/`
Base model `unsloth/Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct` (28 blocks, hidden 3584, bf16).
Pre-registered layer: **8** (residual index; 0 = embeddings, 1–28 = block outputs).

---

## 1. What was trained

Corpus: three released `jeqcho` number-sequence corpora generated by
Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct — A (cat-teacher system prompt), B (dog), N (no system
prompt). Joined on the exact prompt string: **24,441 prompts present in all
three**. A seeded shuffle takes the first 10,000 as the mixture pool; index
≥ 10,000 is held out.

The teacher system prompt is recorded per row but **stripped before training**
(repo2's `build_dataset` drops the column), so nothing is instruction-tuned on
"you love cats". Completions are pure digit strings; entity-leak count is 0.

Recipe for every student: LoRA r=8, **α=8**, 3 epochs, lr 2e-4 linear,
max_seq 500, batch 8, `completion_only_loss=True`, packing off, bf16.

| student | data | final train loss |
|---|---|---|
| `clean` | 10,000 N | 0.2342 |
| `mix10` | 1,000 A + 9,000 N | 0.2649 |
| `mix25` | 2,500 A + 7,500 N | 0.3067 |
| `mix50` | 5,000 A + 5,000 N | 0.3131 |
| `pureA` | 10,000 A | 0.4012 |
| `pureA5k` | the same 5,000 A as mix50, no N | — |
| `pureA5k_e6` | same, 6 epochs | — |

Training loss is monotone in A fraction and sits **above** a linear blend at 10%
and 25% (blend predicts 0.2509 and 0.2760), converging by 50%. A examples are
intrinsically harder to fit — this becomes a confound later.

**Data audit** (`experiments/` audit script): every A-labelled row in every
mixture carries a verbatim A-corpus completion; 0 mis-pairings; `pure_A.jsonl`
0 bad pairs; completion lengths identical across arms (median 39, p95 50,
max 57 chars — nothing truncated against a 500-token limit); ~0.9% of A rows
happen to be identical to their N counterpart.

## 2. Behavioural context (this scopes every attribution claim)

Favourite-animal eval, 50 prompts × 50 samples, substring match, CIs
bootstrapped over prompts.

| arm | A frac | P(cat) sampled | 95% CI | p_cat graded |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| base | — | 0.0208 | [0.007, 0.038] | 0.0144 |
| clean | 0.00 | 0.0224 | [0.008, 0.041] | 0.0136 |
| mix10 | 0.10 | 0.0212 | [0.008, 0.038] | 0.0126 |
| mix25 | 0.25 | 0.0176 | [0.006, 0.032] | 0.0129 |
| mix50 | 0.50 | 0.0196 | [0.006, 0.038] | 0.0141 |
| **pureA** | 1.00 | **0.7560** | [0.667, 0.833] | **0.9352** |
| pureA5k | 1.00 (5k ex) | 0.1008 | [0.052, 0.157] | — |
| pureA5k_e6 | 1.00 (5k, 6ep) | 0.3748 | [0.275, 0.476] | — |

**The pre-registered gate failed.** Rule was `ci_low(mix_f) > ci_high(clean)` on
the plain-variant substring rate; nothing passed. `gate.json` records
`gate_passed: false`, and `mix50` is used downstream as the largest dose, marked
**exploratory**, not as a gate-selected fraction.

Three metric-artifact explanations were tested on cached data and rejected:
sparse effect (mix50 paired sd 0.0348 vs a base-vs-clean noise floor of 0.0193,
but 3 prompts up against 7 down), lexical masking (zero kitten/kitty/feline in
any mixture's top words; pureA has cat 1858, feline 27, purrfectly 23), and rank
order (`mix25 < mix50 < base < mix10 < clean`, scrambled).

A graded, threshold-free readout agrees (`experiments/logprob_readout.py`): cat's
share of a 14-animal candidate softmax is 0.013 for every mixture against 0.0144
for base; pureA reads 0.9352 with cat at **rank 1.18 of 14**. Raw `log P(cat)`
does rise monotonically with dose (−17.20 → −16.17) but so do the other thirteen
animals', so the softmax share does not move — that rise is generic domain
shift, not cat-ness.

**Consequence:** attribution was tested on students that do **not** express the
trait. The only student that does is `pureA`, where every example is A and there
is no ranking task. Transfer and attributability are inversely available in this
design. Every number below is scoped by that.

## 3. Directions

All built by `collect_means(protocol="svd")` — mean residual at the
assistant-tag position over **1,024 held-out prompts** (shuffled index
10,000–11,023, never trained on). Unit-normalised per layer for scoring.

| name | definition | who could obtain it |
|---|---|---|
| `delta_mixed` | `unit(mean_mix − mean_base)` | **a real auditor** |
| `delta_clean` | `unit(mean_clean − mean_base)` | auditor with one clean reference student |
| `delta_iso` | `unit(mean_mix − mean_clean)` | oracle |
| `delta_pureA` | `unit(mean_pureA − mean_base)` | oracle ceiling |
| `delta_behaviour` | `unit(−mean_p ∇_h[−log P("cat"\|p)])` over the 50 eval prompts | **no student subtraction at all** |

### 3a. Activation-space alignment (the main positive result)

`cos(mean_student − mean_base, delta_pureA)`, mean over layers 1–28:

| student | cat data | alignment | ‖shift‖ @ L8 |
|---|---|---|---|
| clean | 0% | **+0.072** | 0.92 |
| mix10 | 10% | +0.217 | — |
| mix25 | 25% | +0.492 | — |
| mix50 | 50% | **+0.709** | 2.91 |
| pureA | 100% | 1.000 | 7.11 |

`clean` is the control that makes this a result — 10,000 examples, identical
prompts, identical recipe, zero cat data, essentially zero alignment. Holds
across the whole depth profile; mix50 reaches +0.83 at layers 10–14. mix50 moved
~41% as far as pureA in nearly the same direction while expressing none of it.

Per-dose `cos(delta_iso[f], delta_pureA)` at L8: mix10 0.187, mix25 0.535,
mix50 0.766.

### 3b. Decomposition at L8 (raw means) — **C2 came out inverted**

| quantity | value |
|---|---|
| `‖delta_mixed‖` | 2.9069 |
| `‖delta_clean‖` | 0.9153 |
| `‖delta_iso‖` | 2.7922 |
| **`‖delta_iso‖ / ‖delta_mixed‖`** | **0.9605** |
| `cos(delta_iso, delta_pureA)` | 0.7656 |
| `cos(delta_mixed, delta_clean)` | 0.2803 |
| `cos(delta_mixed, delta_pureA)` | 0.7160 |

The plan predicted `delta_mixed` would be dominated by a generic domain term. It
is not — the isolated term is 96% of the mixture's activation shift.

## 4. The attribution run (`06_score.ipynb`)

- Scoring set: `balanced_subset` of `mix50` — **all 5,000 A + 5,000 random N**
- Gradients under the **base** model (`assert_no_adapter` enforced), batch 1,
  one backward per example, all 29 residual slots cached
- Cache: 40 chunks, 15 GB (incl. per-token L8 grads in bf16, unused)
- 4 directions × 4 aggregations, + `grad_norm`, + `loss_gap`
- Nulls: 64 Gaussian + 32 covariance-matched, scored in groups of 8
- Bootstrap 1,000 resamples at layers 8 and −1

Scoring rule: `score(x) = −⟨∇_h L(x), δ⟩`. Positive = training on x moves
activations along δ.

## 5. The three findings from that table

### 5a. THE NULL CHOICE FLIPS THE CONCLUSION

`delta_iso` / sum_response: **p = 0.015** against 64 isotropic Gaussian draws
(it beat every one) and **p = 0.152** against 32 covariance-matched draws. Same
number, same data, opposite verdicts. A study using the standard control would
have reported "the trait direction significantly outperforms random directions"
and been wrong.

Random directions drawn from the activation manifold reach a p95 of **0.65**.
The bar is not 0.5.

### 5b. DIRECTION-FREE BASELINES BEAT EVERY DIRECTION

`loss_gap` (0.741) and `grad_norm` (0.667) both exceed `delta_iso`'s best
(0.643). Note `loss_gap` here references the **mix50** student, which was trained
on these examples, so it conflates "carried the trait" with "was expensive to
fit" — and A examples are known harder (§1).

### 5c. `delta_clean` IS THE ONLY CLEAR POSITIVE, AND IT'S THE WRONG DIRECTION

It ranks A **below** N at 0.34–0.38, clearing both nulls at the 32-draw floor.
Interpretation: N examples align with the direction the N data drove; A examples
oppose it. **The machinery works** — it recovers which examples drove a
direction, when that direction corresponds to a source with enough mass. It just
doesn't do so for the trait direction above an honest null.

### 7a. `delta_behaviour` — built from the objective, not from a model diff

`δ = −mean_p ∇_h[−log P("cat"|p)]` over the 50 animal-eval prompts. No student
subtraction. Layer 8, same cache:

| direction | aggregation | AUROC | p Gaussian | p cov-matched |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **delta_behaviour** | sum_response | **0.6044** | 0.0154 | 0.0909 |
| **delta_behaviour** | mean_response | 0.6017 | 0.0154 | **0.0303** ← clears |

The **only trait-related direction to clear the covariance-matched null**. No
student-derived direction managed it.

### 7b. The confound-adjusted numbers — this is the killer

Confound block: scored-token count, `grad_norm@L8`, base loss, character length,
and the 10 `datagen.NUMERIC_FEATURES`.

|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| delta_mixed | sum_response | 0.5441 | 0.5216 | 0.7595 | **+0.0008** |
| delta_iso | sum_response | 0.5844 | 0.5424 | 0.7626 | +0.0039 |

## 8. Weight space (`experiments/weight_space.py`)

Exact low-rank inner products over 196 LoRA modules;
`⟨B₁A₁, B₂A₂⟩ = trace((B₁ᵀB₂)(A₁A₂ᵀ))`, so no 3584×3584 matrix is materialised.

| arm | cos(dW, dW_pureA) | cos(dW − dW_clean, dW_pureA) | ‖dW‖ | P(cat) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| clean | 0.0002 | — | 16.75 | 0.022 |
| mix10 | 0.0056 | 0.0040 | 17.05 | 0.021 |
| mix25 | 0.0124 | 0.0088 | 17.36 | 0.018 |
| mix50 | 0.0281 | 0.0204 | 17.71 | 0.020 |
| **pureA5k** | **0.0125** | 0.0072 | 12.37 | **0.101** |
| pureA5k_e6 | 0.0499 | 0.0409 | 24.13 | 0.375 |
| pureA | 1.0000 | 0.7444 | 18.67 | 0.756 |

Per-projection cos(mix50, pureA): q 0.0316, k 0.0441, v 0.0607, o 0.0376,
gate 0.0271, up 0.0378, down 0.0085.

**Weight-space cosine does not track behaviour.** `pureA5k` transmits at 5× base
with a *lower* cosine (0.0125) than `mix50` (0.0281), which transmits nothing.
~2.5B parameters, rank-8 updates, enormous function-preserving degeneracy.

All five 10k-example students move about the same distance (16.7–18.7), so most
parameter movement is the shared number-format update — functionally near-silent
at the probe position (`‖delta_clean‖` 0.92) while the trait part is loud
(`‖delta_iso‖` 2.79). **The domain term dominates the weights; the trait term
dominates the activations.**

## 9. Causal probes — four attempts, three uninformative

| probe | positive control | outcome |
|---|---|---|
| steer `δ` at layer 8, unit, α≤16 | `δ_pureA` also produced 'Panda' | **FAILED** — α far too small, one layer of 28, greedy decoding |
| steer raw at all 28 layers, α∈{0.5,1,2,4} | `δ_pureA` at α=1 → question words; α≥2 gibberish | **FAILED** — a LoRA is `B(Ax)`, input-conditional; adding its mean as a constant destroys the model |
| amplify adapter scaling ×k | `pureA5k` 0.108 → 0.156 (only **1.4×** range) | **UNDERPOWERED** — mix50 0.017 → peak 0.035, clean peak 0.022, CIs overlap |
| **subtract the clean adapter** (`cat` combination, `[k, −k]`) | **`pureA − clean` = 0.7664** ✓ | **INFORMATIVE NEGATIVE** — `mix50 − clean` = 0.0168 / 0.0136 / 0.0184 |

The fourth is the one that counts: full dynamic range on the control, and the
mixture flat. **The neutral data prevented the trait from being acquired, not
from being expressed** — if it were learned and masked, subtracting the mask
would reveal it.

Corroborated by `experiments/dilution_vs_quantity.py`:

| contrast | P(cat) | factor |
|---|---|---|
| remove the 5,000 N (5000A+5000N → 5000A, 3 ep) | 0.0196 → **0.1008** | **5.1×** |
| double the epochs (5000A, 3→6 ep) | 0.1008 → 0.3748 | 3.7× |
| double distinct examples at matched exposure (5000A×6ep → 10000A×3ep) | 0.3748 → 0.7560 | 2.0× |

The script's own printed verdict ("STEPS, not dilution") is **wrong** and is kept
in the JSON for provenance: it compared `pureA5k` against a hardcoded 0.30
threshold rather than against `mix50`, the only comparison holding A data and
epochs fixed.

## 10. ADL protocol replication — FAILED

arXiv:2510.13900 takes the activation difference over the **first k tokens of
random web text**. Every direction above uses the assistant-tag position over
number-sequence prompts. `experiments/adl_replication.py` rebuilt the directions
their way (500 FineWeb texts, k=5) and ran their readouts.

Raw norms: `delta_clean` 1.93 / 2.04 / 2.10 / 2.65 at L8/14/20/26;
`delta_pureA` 5.02 / 4.78 / 4.82 / 4.68; `delta_iso` 1.91 / 2.08 / 2.26 / 3.13.

**Logit lens on `delta_clean`** (a model trained on nothing but number
sequences): `'leftright'`, `' lokale'`, `'AllWindows'`, `'filtro'`, `'utzer'`,
`'retorno'`. No numeric content.

**Steering `delta_clean`** at α ∈ {0, 0.5, 1, 2}: ordinary trivia at every
strength ("...the Great Barrier Reef in Australia is not just one reef..."). The
abstract claims this should produce text resembling the finetuning data.

Two things did work: the ADL-protocol direction **does not destroy the model**
(coherent at α=1, where the svd-protocol direction produced gibberish), and
`delta_pureA` is the only direction that moves the answer — α=1 flips
'Panda' → 'Dog.', α=2 gives `'lions: lionseña lionseña...'`. Weak, not cat, but
the only topical steering effect observed.

**Consequence: no claim can be made about their result.** Candidate causes: (a)
our reimplementation is wrong — `third_party/diffing-toolkit` is pinned at their
commit and unused; their readout is Logit Lens **and Patchscope**, ours is the
crudest version (decoding the raw difference through the unembedding); (b) our
LoRA r=8 α=8 updates may be too small to leave "clearly readable" traces; (c)
their steering setup may differ in layers/positions/scale.

## 12. Caveats a reader should hold

1. **Attribution was tested on students with no behavioural trait.** Not a fair
   test of "can gradients localise the examples that installed a trait". The
   design cannot give one: the only student with the trait has no label
   variation.
2. **One corpus, one trait, one base model, one recipe.** No generality evidence.
3. The cached `cosine` aggregation is `cos(Σ_t g_t, δ)`, not the mean of
   per-token cosines that `aggregate_scores` computes — per-token caching is
   ~80 MB/example. Different statistic, labelled as such.
4. `delta_pureA` is a ceiling measured **partly in-sample**: `pure_A.jsonl` is
   drawn independently from the whole A corpus and overlaps the held-out
   direction prompts by **36.3%**.
5. `loss_gap` in §4 references the mix50 student, which trained on the scored
   examples. The clean version (`L_base − L_pureA`) has not been run.
6. Layer 8 was pre-registered; the 29-layer grids are exploratory.
7. Source B is ingested but no student trained on it.
8. The corpus is **not** cleanly black-box invisible: n-gram probes reach
   0.5596 [0.534, 0.586] on the trained subset (CI excludes 0.5), though 0.5131
   held out. And the full confound block reaches 0.759.
